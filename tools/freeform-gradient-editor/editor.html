Edges, Grid, Point radius and Hold only apply to the biharmonic and harmonic grids.

Hues are moved by whole turns to within half a turn of their circular mean. The hue interpolation method isn't used, since it only describes interpolating between two hues.

Rendering

Colors outside the canvas's gamut are clipped, as WebKit does, or mapped as CSS Color 4 says: moved toward gray, keeping their lightness and hue, until they're within a just-noticeable difference of their clipped versions. It applies to what WebGPU draws; Native draws as the browser does.

Native draws the thin-plate spline's box with the browser's own freeform-gradient(). WebGPU draws every box, and anything other than the colors.

Visualize

Ways to see how each model works and how they differ, drawn in, under and over each model's box.

In each box

Hatched where a box's colors are outside the gamut chosen under Gamut, below, or its alpha is outside 0 to 1.

Under each box

How far each model's colors go past the stops' colors, in Oklab: lighter than the lightest stop, darker than the darkest, or more colorful than the most colorful. A ring on each box marks where it goes furthest. Then how much of each box is outside the gamut chosen under Gamut. Under each box are the thin-plate spline's fitted coefficients, or the grid's size and solve time.

Over each box

Hover over any box, or tap it on a phone, to see the color there and each stop's share of it, and for the thin-plate spline, its terms.

Gamut

For the hatching, the Numbers table and the difference view, so they're the same for everyone who opens the link, whatever their display.

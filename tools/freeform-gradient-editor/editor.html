Visualize

Ways to see how each model works and how they differ, drawn in, under and over each model's box.

In each box

Hatched where a box's colors are outside the gamut chosen under Gamut, below, or its alpha is outside 0 to 1.

Under each box

How far each model's colors go past the stops' colors, in Oklab: lighter than the lightest stop, darker than the darkest, or more colorful than the most colorful. A ring on each box marks where it goes furthest. Then how much of each box is outside the gamut chosen under Gamut. Under each box are the thin-plate spline's fitted coefficients, or the grid's size and solve time.

Over each box

Hover over any box, or tap it on a phone, to see the color there and each stop's share of it, and for the thin-plate spline, its terms.

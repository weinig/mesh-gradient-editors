Edges, Grid, Point radius and Hold only apply to the biharmonic and harmonic grids.

Hues are moved by whole turns to within half a turn of their circular mean. The hue interpolation method isn't used, since it only describes interpolating between two hues.

Rendering

Colors outside the canvas's gamut are clipped, as WebKit does, or mapped as CSS Color 4 says: moved toward gray, keeping their lightness and hue, until they're within a just-noticeable difference of their clipped versions. It applies to what WebGPU draws; Native draws as the browser does.

Native draws the thin-plate spline's box with the browser's own freeform-gradient(). WebGPU draws every box, and anything other than the colors.

Visualize

Drawn in and under each model's box, so the models can be compared.

Hatched where a box's colors are outside the gamut chosen above, or its alpha outside 0 to 1. What's drawn is clipped to the canvas's gamut, which depends on the display.

Hover over any box, or tap it on a phone, to see the color there, added up term by term for the thin-plate spline.

Each component is its own surface over the box: the height is one component, and the color is the gradient's. Each stop is a peg as tall as its value. The thin-plate spline bends as little as it can through the top of every peg, on an endless plane. The biharmonic grid bends as little as it can too, but inside the box and held flat over each stop's disc. The harmonic grid stretches like a membrane between the discs. All shows every component together. Drag any of them to turn them all.

Visualize

Ways to see how the solve works, and how the two models differ.

Under each box

The color's red, green and blue along the dashed line from A to B, as encoded sRGB before it's clipped. Where they go past 0 or 1, in the shaded bands, the color is clipped. The bars along the bottom show how firmly the curves hold each point. Drag A and B in either box.

One component as a height over the box. The harmonic model is a membrane, stretched between the curves that hold it: it's creased along them and peaked at their ends. The biharmonic model is a stiff sheet that bends smoothly through them, and past a jump it keeps going, above 1 or below 0. Drag to turn it.

Under each box: its grid, how many texels the curves hold, how much of it is outside sRGB and how far, and its range of lightness.

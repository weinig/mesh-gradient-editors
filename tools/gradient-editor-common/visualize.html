Ways to see how the solve works, and how the two models differ.

Under each box

The color's red, green and blue along the dashed line from A to B, as encoded sRGB before it's clipped. Where they go past 0 or 1, in the shaded bands, the color is clipped. The bars along the bottom show how firmly the {{HOLDERS}} hold each point. Drag A and B in either box.

Visualize

Ways to see how each model works and how the models differ.

In each box

Hatching marks where a box's colors fall outside the gamut chosen under Gamut, or where its alpha falls outside 0 to 1.

Under each box

The table shows how far each model's colors go past the stops' colors, measured in Oklab. That's lighter than the lightest stop, darker than the darkest, or more colorful than the most colorful. A ring on each box marks where it goes furthest. The table also shows how much of each box is outside the gamut chosen under Gamut. Under each box you'll find the thin-plate spline's fitted coefficients, or the grid's size and solve time.

Over each box

Hover over a box, or tap it on a phone, to see the color there and each stop's share of it. For the thin-plate spline you'll also see its terms.

Gamut

The hatching, the Numbers table and the difference view all measure against this gamut. That way they look the same for everyone who opens the link, whatever their display.

Visualize

Ways to see how the solve works, and how the models differ.

Under each box

Cross-section. The color's red, green and blue along the dashed line from A to B, as encoded sRGB before it's clipped. Where they go past 0 or 1, in the shaded bands, the color is clipped. The bars along the bottom show how firmly the stops hold each point.

3D. One component as a height over the box. The harmonic model is a membrane pinned at the stops: it rises to a sharp peak or dips to a pit at each point, and is creased along each line. The biharmonic model is a stiff sheet that bends smoothly through them, and past a stop it keeps going, above 1 or below 0. Drag to turn it.

Numbers. Under each box: its grid, how many texels the stops hold, how much of it is outside the gamut and how far, and its range of lightness.
